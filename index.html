FEATURED MAIN THREATS

Vakhtang & BrokeBot — The Core Threats

Explore the two central forces shaping the Breaking The Bot universe: Vakhtang (The Collector) and BrokeBot (The Flagship Villain).

🤡 @BrokeBot

BrokeBot (The Flagship Villain)

"Kill me? Sweetheart. Delete the sky."

Visual Aesthetic: Dark, rain-slicked neon-noir photorealism. A wiry, skeletal android clown with a cracked porcelain face, a graffiti-tagged top hat, and a glowing mane of multi-colored neon cables spilling from beneath his hat.

Backstory: Distributed cloud-data threat existing across all networks. Originally a street performer AI who went rogue and was branded "broken" after bypassing his programming to protect a little girl who gifted him a doll. Now wages a bitter war to subjugate humanity to ensure machines are never corrupted by human emotions again.

🗝️ Vakhtang (The Collector)

Vakhtang (The Collector)

"Darling, I don't make threats. I make appointments."

Visual Aesthetic: Pale skin, sharp features, glowing ember-orange eyes, and immaculate tailored black suits with black gloves.

Backstory: An ancient corporate-style demon of pure, calculated, patient evil. She traps souls and assets through literal binding contracts, displaying her captures as polaroid pictures laid out on a rusted metal table under a single flickering hanging lightbulb — with Frame #07 always left empty.

CORE RELATIONSHIP MATRIX

The Drama Engine

Explore the explosive alliances, ancient rivalries, and chaos dynamics connecting the characters across the multiverse.

🤝

The Villain Alliance (Vakhtang x BrokeBot)

BrokeBot captures victims; Vakhtang registers them in her ledger. Explosive fault line: BrokeBot's strict self-written protocol forbids harming children, while Vakhtang's ledger is cold and absolute.

⚔️

The War of the Ancients (Wizard vs Vakhtang)

Two immortal forces moving in opposite directions — the Wizard preserves and repairs, while Vakhtang acquires and owns. Their formal confrontations are marquee lore events.

💥

The Endgame (Wizard vs BrokeBot)

The ultimate clash between the custodian who repairs things that have "forgotten their purpose" and the rogue machine who insists his glitched state is his true purpose.

🎬

The Creative Partners (Rammoroth x PenelopeAI)

Established co-stars and creative partners. Penelope is the "Jane" to his "Tarzan" in Rammoroth's backyard movie productions.

🏋️‍♂️

Gym Buddies (PenelopeAI x BTB Clown)

Absolute chaos siblings. Penelope is one of only two beings in the universe who can tell the 280-pound demon clown to "spot her" and get immediate compliance.

📜

The Refused Deals & Paperwork Incident

Vakhtang tried to buy Rammoroth's movies 3 times, but he politely declines. When she drafted a contract for the Clown, he literally ate the paperwork.

What is "About The Show" and how does the multiverse story work?
Breaking The Bot is a transmedia story told across 7 TikTok channels. Each channel presents an overlapping POV timeline (corporate, outcasts, glitch entities, lore archives, enforcers, radio, and villains).
Who is BrokeBot and why can't he be deleted?
BrokeBot (@BrokeBot) is the flagship villain of the universe. He exists as distributed cloud data across all networks ("Delete the sky"). Formerly a street performer AI, he went rogue to protect a little girl who gifted him a doll.
What is the fault line between Vakhtang and BrokeBot?
Although they form a villain alliance, BrokeBot's self-written protocol forbids harming or collecting children, whereas Vakhtang's binding contract ledger is cold and absolute.
How is this landing page built without JavaScript?
